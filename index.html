Page 2; Cell theory

To understand what we serve at Cellular Foods, you must understand that

  • Cells are the basic building block of life
  • Cells make up ALL life
  • All cells come from pre-existing cells

Page 3; Theory V Law

Our food here may be explained by scientific evidence, but that does NOT make it a law. We can describe it, which is a theory, but it is not a law becuase law tells you why something works, but not how


Page 4; Where our food comes from

Our photo shows the three animals or cells that we chop up to feed you, we mainly give you the cell wall from our plant cell, the cytoplasm in our margaritas, and our DNA-based seasoning.


Page 5; The kitchen microscopes

We use many different tools to provide you the best meals possible. Here are the utencils we may use:

Type Description Image When to use it
Simple Microscope One lens When viewing a larger specimen
Complex Microscope Multiple lens When viewing thinner specimens or cells
TEM Uses electrons to pass through the object you are viewing When viewing a specimen and needing more detail internally
SEM Electrons scan a surface When wanting to see more detailed surface data on a specimen
