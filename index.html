Page 2; Cell theory

To understand what we serve at Cellular Foods, you must understand that

  • Cells are the basic building block of life
  • Cells make up ALL life
  • All cells come from pre-existing cells

Page 3; Theory V Law

Our food here may be explained by scientific evidence, but that does NOT make it a law. We can describe it, which is a theory, but it is not a law becuase law tells you why something works, but not how

Page 8; The actual menu

We have many varieties of food based apon each part of a cell, which is explained

Food Description
Nuclear membrane boiled egg A encapsulated snack, with an inner yolk protected from the rest of the egg. In real cells, the nuclear membrane keeps genetic information put of the cytoplasm.
Cell wall chips A solid, crunchy snack. In real cells, the wall keeps the cell in shape and protects it.
Chromosome burrito DNA tightly packed in a rolled tortilla. In real cells, the chromosome keeps the DNA and safely distributes it during division.
Nucleolus dumpling A thick, DNA filled membrane wrapped in a dumpling (nucleus). In real cells, this produces rDNA and ribosomes, while also contianing genetic information.
Ribosome steak A protien-producing meat entree. In real cells, this produces protien by reading and decoding gentic information (DNA).
Centriole churros A barrel shaped treat. In real cells, these organize cells for cell division.
Cell membrane créme brulee A sugary treat held together by a strong top layer. In real cells, the membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell.
Mitochondria protien bar A high-energy side. In real cells, the midochondria produces ATP, which powers the cell and gives it energy.
Flagellum noodles Thin, noodles in a bowl. In real cells, the Flagellum allows the cell to move (mostly in bacteria though).
Chloroplast jelly beans A sugary capsule dessert. In real cells, this produces glucose for plant cells from carbon-dioxide, water, and sunlight.
lysosome gushers A hard, jelly filled apetizer. In real cells, a lysosome recycles old worn out cell parts.
Vacule coconut A nutrient-filled drink. In real cells, this holds the nutrients and "valuables" for the cell.
Central vacule slushie A thick, dense nutrient filled storage. In real cells, this holds the most nutrients and acts like a permanant storage.
Pili meatball A protien filled organelle that contains DNA. In real cells, these help with adhesion and also reproduction.
Endoplasmic reticulum lasagna Very meaty folds. In real cells, this produces, moves, and folds protiens + lipids.
Golgi body bento box A packaged and contained assortment of protiens. In real cells, this produces and packages protiens and lipids to ship to other parts of the cell.
